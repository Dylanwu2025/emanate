# Zoe — Private Vault (Group Dynamics, Health, Allergies & Pacing)

## Traveler Profiles & Medical / Dietary Restrictions
1. **Sam**:
   - **Severe Shellfish Allergy**: Anaphylactic risk from crustacean/mollusk cross-contamination and **cannot eat at or walk through cramped stalls with shared seafood grills** or heavy shellfish steam. carries an EpiPen.
   - **Motion Sickness**: Gets severe motion sickness on **boats, ferries, and winding bus routes**; smooth rail transit (JR trains, Shinkansen, subway) is completely fine.
2. **Priya**:
   - **Strict Vegetarian**: Eats no meat, poultry, fish, or bonito/dashi stock made from fish flakes; needs verified plant-based / kombu dashi options (tofu kaiseki, vegetarian ramen, temple shojin ryori).
3. **Alex & Jordan**:
   - Flexible eaters, photography enthusiasts, but burn out quickly if more than 2 major sightseeing neighborhoods are packed into a single day.

## Non-Negotiable Group Pacing & Sleep Rules
- **No Waking Before 7:30 AM**: The group agreed unanimously that **no one wakes up before 7:30 AM** on vacation. Any flight departing at 6:45 AM (or requiring an early-morning airport wakeup) is strictly vetoed.
- **Midday Recharge Buffer**: Keep at least a 60-minute low-key cafe or hotel rest window between afternoon walking blocks so the group stays energized for evening dinners.
